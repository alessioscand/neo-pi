Accurate

Powered by Geolith, a highly accurate Neo Geo emulator. Play in MVS arcade mode or AES home-console mode, in all BIOS regions.

Low latency

Gamepads are polled every millisecond. Beam racing keeps video output in sync with the HDMI signal for near-instant response.

Pixel-perfect

Crisp, square pixels at 1080p or 720p, with optional scanlines, lag-free.

Fast boot

A lightweight bare-metal system that gets you into the game in just a few seconds.

Streamlined

Save states, BIOS settings, screenshots, lazy finger (turbo buttons). All driven from the gamepad.

MV-1 mode

Got just one game on your SD card? neo-pi boots straight into it!

Get started in 3 steps

  1. Download the neo-pi package and extract its contents to the root of a FAT32-formatted microSD card.
  2. Copy your neogeo.zip BIOS file and your .neo game files into the neopi folder.
  3. Connect a USB gamepad and an HDMI display (on a Pi 4: HDMI 0, the port next to the power socket), insert the card and power on your Raspberry Pi.

BIOS files and games are not included.

Controls

The first time you connect a pad, neo-pi asks you to press the buttons you want as A, B, C, D, Start and Select. Button hints are shown on screen in the menus. Then, in game:
