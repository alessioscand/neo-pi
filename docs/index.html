Accurate

Powered by Geolith, a highly accurate NEO·GEO emulator. Play in MVS arcade mode or AES home-console mode, in all BIOS regions and UniBIOS.

Fast boot

A lightweight bare-metal system that gets you into the game in just a few seconds. Just one game on your SD card? neo-pi boots straight into it!

Pixel-perfect

With integer scaling at 1080p or 720p, every pixel stays sharp and square. If you miss your old TV, add scanlines or a composite filter.

Highly responsive

Gamepads are polled every millisecond, and neo-pi uses beam racing to send each scanline to the TV as soon as it is rendered, in sync with the display's refresh. An emulator with double buffering instead waits for the entire frame to be rendered, adding up to one frame of latency.

Get started

  1. Download the neo-pi package and extract its contents to the root of a FAT32-formatted microSD card.
  2. Copy your neogeo.zip BIOS file and your .neo game files into the neopi folder.
  3. Connect a USB gamepad and an HDMI display (on a Pi 4: HDMI 0, the port next to the power socket), insert the card and power on your Raspberry Pi.

BIOS files and games are not included.
